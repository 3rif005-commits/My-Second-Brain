Gradient descent minimises the loss by stepping against the gradient. The learning rate sets the step size, and momentum damps oscillation.

Gradient descent: A method used for finding the minimum of a function by iteratively moving in the direction of the steepest descent.

Learning rate: A hyperparameter that controls how much to change the model in response to the estimated error each time the model weights are updated.

Momentum: A technique that helps accelerate gradients vectors in the right directions, thus leading to faster converging.

Loss: A measure of how well or poorly a model is functioning, typically by comparing the model's predictions with the actual outcomes.

Oscillation: The behavior in which the learning process fluctuates back and forth instead of converging towards a minimum.⁠
