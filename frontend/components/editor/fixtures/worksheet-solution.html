Exercise 01 · Laser beam

A laser beam: \lambda = 632.8\,\text{nm}, average power P = 1\,\text{mW}, beam diameter D = 2\,\text{mm}.

Frequency and intensity

Insight: Frequency is how many times per second the field oscillates; the source fixes it, so in glass only the wavelength shrinks.
Intensity is the power through each square metre: a thin beam of modest power can be far more intense than a bulb.

(a) Frequency

Find the frequency of the wave.

Rule: wave relation

c = \lambda f

Answer: f = c/\lambda = 3 \times 10^8 / 632.8 \times 10^{-9} \approx 4.74 \times 10^{14}\,\text{Hz}

(b) Intensity

Find the intensity of the beam.

Rule: intensity of a uniform beam

I = \frac{P}{A} \qquad A = \pi \left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^2
Solution
  1. Beam area → A = \pi (10^{-3})^2 = 3.14 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{m}^2
  2. Intensity → I = 10^{-3} / 3.14 \times 10^{-6} = 318\,\text{W/m}^2

Check: 1\,\text{mW} spread over 3\,\text{mm}^2 gives hundreds of \text{W/m}^2, as expected.

Answer: I \approx 318\,\text{W/m}^2

Trap: D is the diameter: using \pi D^2 makes the area four times too big.

Field amplitudes

Insight: The field amplitudes are how hard the wave pushes on charges; intensity grows with their square, so doubling E_0 quadruples the energy delivered.

Find the amplitudes E_0 and B_0 of the beam's fields.

Rule: intensity of a plane wave, and the field ratio

I = \tfrac12 c \varepsilon_0 E_0^2 \qquad B_0 = \frac{E_0}{c}
Solution
  1. Electric amplitude → E_0 = \sqrt{2I / c\varepsilon_0} = \sqrt{2 \times 318 / (3 \times 10^8 \times 8.85 \times 10^{-12})} \approx 490\,\text{V/m}
  2. Magnetic amplitude → B_0 = 490 / 3 \times 10^8 \approx 1.63 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{T}

Check: substituting back, \tfrac12 c\varepsilon_0 (490)^2 \approx 318\,\text{W/m}^2.

Answer: E_0 \approx 490\,\text{V/m}, B_0 \approx 1.63 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{T}

Exercise 02 · Unit of momentum

Insight: Momentum is motion that resists being stopped: a slow truck and a fast bullet can carry the same amount.

Which is the SI unit of momentum?
A) N · B) kg·m/s · C) J/s · D) kg·m/s²

Rule: definition of momentum

p = m v
Solution
  • A ✗ the newton is the unit of force.
  • B ✓ kg \times m/s.
  • C ✗ J/s is the watt, the unit of power.
  • D ✗ this is the newton again, written out.

Answer: B, kg·m/s
