Q1 · Ball dropped from a roof

Goal: link the height, time and speed of a fall from rest; gravity gives a uniform acceleration starting from zero speed.
A ball is dropped from rest from a 20 m roof; ignore air resistance. (a) How long does it take to land? (b) How fast is it moving when it lands?

(a) Time to fall

Rule: uniform acceleration from rest, s = \tfrac12 g t^2

Solution

  1. Dropped means u = 0, so:

    s = \tfrac12 g t^2

  2. Solve for t and substitute s = 20\,\text{m}, g = 9.8\,\text{m/s}^2:

    t = \sqrt{\frac{2s}{g}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 20}{9.8}} = 2.02\,\text{s}

Check: the units give \sqrt{\text{m}/(\text{m/s}^2)} = \text{s}, and about 2 s for a 20 m fall is plausible.

Answer: t \approx 2.0\,\text{s}

(b) Landing speed

Rule: uniform acceleration, v = u + g t

Answer: v = 0 + 9.8 \times 2.02 \approx 20\,\text{m/s}, downward

Shortcut: v = \sqrt{2gs} gives the landing speed without the time, and checks (a) and (b) together.

Q2 · Unit of momentum

Goal: find a unit from the defining formula of the quantity, since SI units multiply like the quantities do.
Which is the SI unit of momentum?
A) N · B) kg·m/s · C) J/s · D) kg·m/s²

Rule: definition of momentum, p = m v

Solution
  • A ✗ the newton is the unit of force.
  • B ✓ kg × m/s.
  • C ✗ J/s is the watt, the unit of power.
  • D ✗ this is the newton again, written out.

Answer: B, kg·m/s

Trap: A and D are the same unit. When two options are equal, both are wrong.

Q3 · Terminal speed with a parachute

Goal: explain a steady speed as a balance of forces, where drag grows with speed and area.
Explain why a skydiver falls at a lower terminal speed once the parachute opens.

Rule: Newton's first law at terminal speed, F_{\text{drag}}(v) = m g

Answer: The canopy raises the drag at every speed, so drag balances the weight at a lower speed.

  • Just after opening: drag exceeds weight, so the net force points up and the skydiver slows down.
  • Slowing lowers the drag: until drag equals the weight again, at a lower speed.

Marks: one mark for each link in the chain: area up, drag up, net force up, slows down, balance at a lower speed.
