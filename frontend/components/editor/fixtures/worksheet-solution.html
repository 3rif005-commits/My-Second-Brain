Solutions

Q1 · Ball dropped from a roof

Goal: a fall from rest is uniform acceleration from zero speed

A ball is dropped from rest from a 20\,\text{m} roof; ignore air resistance.

(a) Time to fall

How long does it take to land?

Rule: uniform acceleration from rest

s = \tfrac12 g t^2
Solution
  1. Solve for the time → t = \sqrt{2s/g}
  2. Substitute → t = \sqrt{2 \times 20 / 9.8} = 2.02\,\text{s}

Check: about 2\,\text{s} for a 20\,\text{m} drop is plausible.

Answer: t \approx 2.0\,\text{s}

(b) Landing speed

How fast is it moving when it lands?

Rule: uniform acceleration

v = u + g t

Answer: v = 9.8 \times 2.02 \approx 20\,\text{m/s}, downward

Shortcut: v = \sqrt{2gs} gives the speed without the time, and checks (a) and (b) together.

Q2 · Unit of momentum

Goal: a unit follows from the quantity's defining formula

Which is the SI unit of momentum?
A) N · B) kg·m/s · C) J/s · D) kg·m/s²

Rule: definition of momentum

p = m v
Solution
  • A ✗ the newton is the unit of force.
  • B ✓ kg × m/s.
  • C ✗ J/s is the watt, the unit of power.
  • D ✗ this is the newton again, written out.

Answer: B, kg·m/s

Trap: A and D are the same unit. When two options are equal, both are wrong.

Q3 · Terminal speed with a parachute

Goal: a steady speed means the forces balance

Explain why a skydiver falls at a lower terminal speed once the parachute opens.

Rule: Newton's first law at terminal speed

F_{\text{drag}}(v) = m g

Answer: The canopy raises the drag at every speed, so drag balances the weight at a lower speed.

  • Just after opening: drag exceeds weight, so the net force points up and the skydiver slows down.
  • Slowing lowers the drag: until drag equals the weight again, at a lower speed.

Marks: one mark for each link in the chain: area up, drag up, net force up, slows down, balance at a lower speed.
