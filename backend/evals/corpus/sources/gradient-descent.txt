[section 1]
Gradient descent: iterative optimisation. Start at theta_0, repeatedly step in
the direction of steepest descent of the loss. The step size is the learning
rate, eta.

theta_{t+1} = theta_t - eta * grad L(theta_t)

[section 2]
A learning rate too large diverges; too small crawls. Common schedules: step
decay, cosine annealing, and warmup followed by decay.

Stochastic gradient descent uses a minibatch estimate of the gradient. The
noise it introduces acts as a mild regulariser and helps escape saddle points.

[section 3]
Momentum accumulates an exponentially weighted average of past gradients, which
damps oscillation across ravines. Adam combines momentum with per-parameter
scaling by the second moment estimate.
