================= "Layout" =================
Reached by: Settings → Layout. PUSHES with a back arrow (← Layout ×).

TOP: a 3x3 GRID OF VIEW-TYPE CARDS, each an icon above a label.
  Table (SELECTED — blue border + blue label) | Board    | Timeline
  Calendar                                    | List     | Gallery
  Chart                                       | Feed     | Map

  NINE view types. Form is absent — a Form view is created some other way.
  Our ViewTabs offers TEN in a native <select> (table, board, gallery, list,
  feed, calendar, timeline, chart, form, dashboard). Notion has Map (we cut it,
  deliberately: no geocoding/tile provider) and lacks Dashboard/Form here.

  *** This card grid is ALSO the answer for §7's "+ New view" popover. ***
  The plan described "a grid of view-type cards" for view creation — this is
  what those cards look like: icon above label, 3 per row, selected state is a
  blue border plus blue label text.

THEN: display toggles, each a labelled row with a switch on the right.
--- verbatim ---
Show vertical lines      [toggle ON]
Show page icon           [toggle ON]
Wrap all content         [toggle ON]
Open pages in            Side peek   >
--- end ---

  - "Wrap all content" is the VIEW-LEVEL counterpart of the column header menu's
    per-column "Unwrap content". Both exist; the spec must cover both.
  - "Show page icon" controls the 📄 icon seen in the row's title cell.
  - "Open pages in" carries its current value ("Side peek") and a chevron.

================= "Open pages in" =================
Opens as a POPOVER OVERLAYING the Layout panel, anchored to its row — not a push,
not a docked flyout. A third positioning behaviour inside the sidebar.

--- verbatim ---
▣  Side peek                                        ✓
   Open pages on the side. Keeps the view behind interactive.
   Default for Table                    ← a link/annotation, styled as a link
▤  Center peek
   Open pages in a focused, centered modal.
▥  Full page
   Open pages in full page.
--- end ---

*** RESOLVES TWO TBDs ***

1. The plan's §9 "side peek / center peek / full page" WAS the right set of three
   — it was just at the wrong level. These are the VIEW's default open mode.
   The per-row "Open in" menu offers only New tab / Side peek as an override.

2. "Keeps the view behind interactive" is Notion's OWN COPY confirming the side
   peek is NON-MODAL. Our RowPeek renders a bg-black/30 backdrop over the whole
   viewport and blocks interaction. Direct textual confirmation of the
   behavioural difference recorded in row-peek.txt.

ROW ANATOMY — every row here is icon + label + description line, and the current
one carries a ✓ plus an extra annotation link ("Default for Table").
MenuRow.description confirmed a second time (first: "Show large counts as 99+").
The annotation link is a NEW element — a per-row secondary link, distinct from
`description`. Add `annotation?: {label, href|onSelect}` to MenuRow, or treat it
as part of description with inline markup. Decide in the design doc.

NOTE ON MAPPING TO OUR CONFIG
  All four of these are per-view display settings and belong in `view.config` —
  a JSONB pass-through. No backend change needed for any of them.
