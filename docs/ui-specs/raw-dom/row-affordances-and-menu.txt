================= ROW HOVER (§9) =================
Hovering a row reveals FIVE affordances, in two groups:

  OUTSIDE the table, in the left gutter (x≈299..356), in this order:
    +      (x≈299)  add a row below
    ⠿      (x≈321)  drag handle — 6-dot grid
    ☐      (x≈356)  selection checkbox
  INSIDE the title cell:
    📄     (x≈390)  the row's page icon
    ▣ OPEN (x≈593..640, right-aligned in the cell)

*** CORRECTIONS TO THE PLAN ***
- The plan called the row menu trigger "⋮⋮". It is the DRAG HANDLE (⠿), and it
  is BOTH the drag grip and the menu trigger — clicking opens the menu, dragging
  reorders. One affordance, two gestures.
- "OPEN" is a LABELLED BUTTON with an icon and the word OPEN, right-aligned
  inside the title cell — not a bare icon. Our OpenNoteButton is icon-only.
- The gutter affordances live OUTSIDE the table's left edge, in a gutter that
  exists only on hover. That means the table needs left padding reserved
  permanently, or rows shift on hover. Our TableView has no such gutter.
- The hovered/selected cell also shows a small circle at its bottom-right corner
  (x≈652,y≈238) — a fill/extend handle. Not in the plan at all. Capture its
  behaviour before speccing; do not assume it is Excel-style drag-fill.

NOTES
- Search-at-top ("Search actions…") again — a third surface with it, after the
  type picker and the sidebar panels. It is Notion's default for any list of
  more than a handful of rows.
- Right-aligned keyboard hints on four rows (Ctrl+⇧+M, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+⇧+P, Del).
  MenuRow.hint confirmed in use a second time.
- Two rows have sub-panels: "Edit property" and "Open in".
- Footer region carries last-edited metadata — MenuPanel.footer confirmed again.
- "Move to Trash", not "Delete". Our row deletion goes through
  DELETE /api/notes/{id}, which is a soft-delete to trash — the vocabulary
  already matches. Good.
- "Duplicate" is offered here for a ROW. Our backend has no row-duplicate
  endpoint that copies the page body (gap B4 in the plan). Confirmed as needed.

================= BULK SELECTION BAR (§9 shot 61) =================
Clicking the drag handle ALSO SELECTED the row. This is worth noting on its own:
the drag handle is a third gesture target — it selects, opens a menu, and drags.

On selection:
  - checkboxes appear in BOTH the header row and every row
  - the header's checkbox becomes select-all
  - a floating toolbar replaces the view toolbar area:
        [ 1 selected ]  [≡] [#] [⊙] [☰] [⊙] [⊙]  [🗑]  [⋯]
    The middle icons are PROPERTY-TYPE icons — i.e. bulk-edit a property across
    the selection. Then a trash, then an overflow ⋯.
  - the selected row is tinted
