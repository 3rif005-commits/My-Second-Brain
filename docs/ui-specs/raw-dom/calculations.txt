*** "Edit property" is present on Number and ABSENT on Text. ***

--- Number column menu, verbatim ---

→|  Unwrap content
  ── divider ──
|←  Insert left
→|  Insert right                      ← rendered greyed here

  "Edit property" appears for types that HAVE per-type configuration (Number has
  a number format; Text has none). This is a THIRD dimension of the type-
  dependent row set, on top of title-vs-ordinary.
  → The header menu's rows are: base rows + type-conditional rows. Model it that
    way, not as three hardcoded arrays.
  → "Insert right" greyed on the last-but-one column is OBSERVED, cause not
    established. Do not assert a rule.
