The original observation came from the ACCESSIBILITY TREE of an empty database,
where they are present as buttons regardless of visual state — read_page reports
them whether or not they are visually revealed. That is a general hazard of the
a11y-tree method and is worth remembering: **the tree tells you an element
exists, not that it is visible.** Hover-revealed affordances must be confirmed
from a screenshot, never from read_page alone.

For §8's spec: the header's three affordances follow the same reserved-space
hover pattern as the row gutter (§9) — they must not shift the title when they
appear.
