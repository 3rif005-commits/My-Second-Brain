Where does the AI live in the app?

You said "the user should be able to call it everywhere". Here are four ways "everywhere" can work. Pick the one that feels closest to what you want — we'll refine from there.

A

Always-on side panel

Persistent chat docked to the right (like Cursor / Notion AI). You're always one glance away. The panel knows which note you're on.

📄 My Note
🧠 Chat
B

Floating launcher (⌘K spotlight)

Hidden until you call it. Press ⌘K anywhere → modal pops over the page, you ask, AI streams the answer, you close it. Great for quick questions without leaving your flow.

⚡ Ask AI… ⌘K
C

Inline AI inside the editor

Type /ai in any block → AI writes into the note itself, block by block, streaming. The note is the conversation. Selecting text + ⌘J asks AI about the selection.

📄 Calculus — Chain Rule
⚡ /ai give me a worked example
D

All three — one shared conversation

Side panel + ⌘K + inline /ai are all doors into the same persistent thread. Ask via ⌘K, follow up in the panel, drop the answer into the note inline. The thread remembers everything.

📄 Note
⚡ inline
🧠 Thread
⚡ ⌘K — Ask anything
